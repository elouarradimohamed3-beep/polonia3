A stuttering picture, a loading circle and short freezes are the most common problems when watching internet television. In most cases the cause is in the home network, not in the service. Below are nine steps that solve most problems. Start with the first and move on until the picture is smooth.

## 1. Check your connection speed

Run an internet speed test on the device you watch on. Look at the download result. For HD you need roughly 8 to 10 Mb/s per device, and for 4K about 25 Mb/s. You will find more exact values in the article [What internet speed do you need for IPTV](/en/blog/internet-speed-for-iptv).

## 2. Use a cable or the 5 GHz band

Wi-Fi is the most frequent culprit. If you can, connect the box or TV with an Ethernet cable. If a cable is not possible, join the 5 GHz network and move the router closer to the device.

## 3. Restart the router and the device

Unplug the router for about 30 seconds, plug it in again and wait for all the lights to settle. Also restart the TV, box or phone. This simple step removes many temporary errors.

## 4. Limit other use of the network

Big file downloads, game updates, cloud backups and video calls take bandwidth. Pause them while you watch and see if the picture is smoother.

## 5. Change the DNS servers

Sometimes setting public DNS servers instead of your provider's helps. You can enter for example **1.1.1.1** and **1.0.0.1**, or **8.8.8.8** and **8.8.4.4**. You find the setting in the network options of the device or router. If you see no difference, restore the previous settings.

## 6. Update the app and clear its cache

An old version of the app or a full cache can cause freezing. Check whether an update is available. On Android and Fire TV devices you can open **Settings**, **Applications**, choose the app and click **Clear cache**. If that does not help, reinstall the app.

## 7. Lower the picture quality or choose another stream

If the connection is on the edge, lower the quality in the app settings, for example from 4K to Full HD. Many apps also let you choose another server or version of a channel. A smooth HD picture is better than a stuttering 4K one.

## 8. Check whether a VPN slows the connection

If you use a VPN, switch it off for a moment and check the picture. A VPN adds delay and can lower speed, especially at peak hours. If it is better with the VPN off, try another VPN server or watch without it.

## 9. Check whether the problem is one channel or one time of day

Look for a pattern:

- **One channel** stutters while the others work? The problem may be in that particular broadcast.
- **Everything** stutters in the evening? Your provider's network is probably overloaded then, or your line is too slow.
- **One device** stutters? Focus on its app and its connection to the network.

Write down when and where the problem happens. It helps a lot in solving the matter.
