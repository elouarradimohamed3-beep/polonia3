Polonia, meaning Poles living outside Poland, has always looked for a way to keep a piece of home in the living room: the news, series, children's programmes and football. Today the simplest way is television over the internet. This guide explains what IPTV Polonia is, how to start in a quarter of an hour and what to keep in mind so that watching stays comfortable.

## IPTV Polonia in a nutshell

IPTV stands for Internet Protocol Television. The programme reaches you through your internet connection instead of an aerial, a satellite dish or a cable. You watch it in an app on a smart TV, phone, tablet, computer or a streaming stick such as a Fire TV Stick.

**IPTV Polonia** is a service for people who want to watch Polish television and on-demand programmes where they live. You choose a plan for as long as you need, from one day to two years, and for 1 to 5 screens. You may also see it searched for as IPTV Polska.

## How to start in 15 minutes

1. **Choose a plan.** Decide how many screens and for how long. To begin you can take a short period or ask for a [free trial](/en#trial).
2. **Place your order.** Pay with PayPal or a card, or message us on WhatsApp.
3. **Receive your login details.** We send them by e-mail, usually within 5 minutes to 6 hours after payment. Check your spam folder too.
4. **Install the app.** Choose your device in the [setup guide](/en/setup-guide) and follow a few steps.
5. **Check.** Switch on a few channels and check the picture and sound. If something is wrong, write to support.

## What you need

- **Devices.** A smart TV (Samsung, LG, Android TV), Fire TV Stick, phone or tablet, computer or a MAG box.
- **Internet.** As a rule of thumb, about 8 to 10 Mb/s per device for HD and about 25 Mb/s for 4K. If several people watch at home the numbers add up. We describe the details in [What internet speed do you need for IPTV](/en/blog/internet-speed-for-iptv).
- **A subscription.** You receive login details after you order.

## The most common beginner mistakes

- **2.4 GHz Wi-Fi through three walls.** If you can, use a cable or the 5 GHz band.
- **Too few screens in the plan.** Count every device that may be on at the same time.
- **A space in the username or password.** It is easy to add an invisible space when copying. Type the details carefully.
- **An old TV.** A very slow smart TV is often the reason for freezing. A Fire TV Stick often solves it.
- **Expecting miracles on a weak line.** Even the best service cannot improve a connection that is too slow. See [9 fixes for buffering](/en/blog/iptv-buffering-causes-and-fixes).

## Family and children

Plans cover up to 5 devices at the same time, so a child can watch cartoons in the bedroom while the adults watch the news in the living room. Many IPTV apps offer parental controls with a PIN. Set it up before you hand the tablet to a child.

## What to check when choosing a provider

Before you pay, check a few things:

- **A free trial.** A good service lets you test it on your device.
- **Clear prices.** You should know what you pay for the period and number of devices.
- **Support in your language.** Help in Polish can be priceless.
- **Refund rules.** Read them before buying.
- **The approach to copyright.** IPTV is a legal technology, but what matters is whether the service has the rights to the programmes it offers. A good provider has a page where rights holders can contact it. Ours is the [copyright policy](/en/copyright-policy).
- **No unrealistic promises.** Offers that seem too good to be true usually are.
