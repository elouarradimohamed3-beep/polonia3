One of the largest Polish communities in Europe lives in the United Kingdom. Many people rent flats or rooms, move often and do not want to fit a satellite dish. In that situation television over the internet has plenty of advantages. Below are practical tips for people in the UK.

## The time difference

The United Kingdom is **one hour behind Poland**. A programme that starts at 20:00 in Poland appears at 19:00 for you. Keep that in mind for football and the news, and set the right time zone in the app so the TV guide (EPG) shows local times.

## Why IPTV suits life in the UK

- **Renting.** In many rented flats and shared houses permission to fit a dish is hard to get. An app needs no permission.
- **Moving.** You take your account with you. All you need is internet.
- **Several rooms, several screens.** A plan for 2 to 5 devices lets you watch in different rooms at the same time.
- **A flexible period.** You can start with a few days or a month and extend when you are happy.

## Internet at home

Before you order a plan, check your connection speed on the device you will watch on. As a rule of thumb you need about 8 to 10 Mb/s per device in HD. In shared houses the Wi-Fi is often overloaded, so if you can, connect the TV with a cable or place the stick close to the router. There is more in [What internet speed do you need for IPTV](/en/blog/internet-speed-for-iptv).

## Which devices

Most newer smart TVs have an app store. If yours is older or very slow, buy a Fire TV Stick and use the guide [IPTV on Fire TV Stick](/en/blog/iptv-on-fire-tv-stick-setup). A box from your TV or broadband provider usually does not let you install your own apps, so you need a separate device or a smart TV.

## Payment

We list prices in euros. If you pay by card in pounds, your bank converts the amount at its own rate and may add a currency conversion fee. Ask your bank. We accept PayPal, including Visa or Mastercard through PayPal.

1. Ask for a [free trial](/en#trial) and check it on your TV or phone.
2. Choose a [plan](/en#plans) for the number of screens in your home.
3. Install the app with the [setup guide](/en/setup-guide).
4. If something does not work, [message us](/en/contact). We help in Polish and English.

## A few last tips

- Tell your housemates that you will use the internet for watching. Big downloads at the same time can worsen the picture.
